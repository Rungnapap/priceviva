## Phase 1 — Location and Local Deal Radar ✅ (🧪 live lookups need internet)
- **Places:** any real town or postcode, e.g. Kalmar or "392 32", via OpenStreetMap Nominatim behind `/api/geocode`:
  - 1 request/second across the server, 20 per minute per visitor, 24 h cache;
  - identifying User-Agent (`GEOCODER_CONTACT`), no API key.
  - Countries searched: SE, DE, GB, NO, DK, FI (`GEOCODER_COUNTRIES`).
- **"Use my location":** asks permission only when tapped, rounds to ~1 km, looks up the town name and shows "Detected: Kalmar" as a suggestion. Nothing changes until the user confirms. It never snaps to another city.
- **Area types kept apart:** detected suggestion, temporary browsing area (this tab), saved default area (this browser).
- **Radius:** 5/10/25/50/100 km, kept across navigation and refresh.
- **Old saved areas** (city-only) still load.
- **Local Deal Radar:**
  - real merchant/verified offers come from a new database function `pa_catalog_local_offers`, with distance from real store coordinates;
  - map pins appear only for real stores, and cards and pins stay in sync;
  - demo stores sit in a separate "Demonstration stores" section behind a switch (on by default only in demo mode), with no distances and no pins;
  - Kalmar shows an honest empty state that names the nearest demo cities.
- **Tests:**
  - geocoder unit tests: Kalmar, postcodes, reverse lookup with rounding, rate limits, failures;
  - "Kalmar stays Kalmar" unit test;
  - database test of local offers within a radius (verified/merchant shown, rejected/expired hidden, ~1 km distance);
  - browser journey: pick Kalmar → header and radar show Kalmar → refresh keeps it → empty state → demo switch.

## Phase 7a — Currencies ✅
- **Display currencies:** SEK, EUR, GBP, USD, NOK, DKK, CHF, CAD, AUD, JPY.
- **Rates:** ECB reference rates (date shown in the currency menu). If ECB is unreachable, a complete table labelled "Demo reference rates" is used instead, so there are never partial or missing rates.
- **Display:**
  - converted prices are marked "≈ converted from EUR";
  - the shop's own currency stays visible in the offer table;
  - JPY shows no decimals;
  - changing currency never changes the delivery country.

## Phase 2 — Global Compare ✅
- **Two separate jobs on `/compare`:**
  - **A · Compare retailer offers:** one exact product across shops;
  - **B · Compare products:** 2–3 different products side by side.
- **Matching:** offers are grouped only by GTIN, or by brand + model + variant + condition. Sizes, colours and conditions are never merged, and the rules are shown on the page.
- **Comparison list:**
  - the counter is hidden when the list is empty;
  - each product has a visible **Remove**, plus **Clear all**;
  - the phone tab bar shows the count.
- **Prices:**
  - the original currency is always shown, and converted totals are marked ≈ "charged in EUR";
  - unknown shipping or import costs show "Not available — not counted as zero";
  - source, last-checked time and stock are shown where the source states them.

## Phase 3 — Real retailer data and trust ✅ (⛔ live feeds need merchant approval)
- **Where data comes from:** `docs/SETUP_REAL_DATA.md` lists what is real, what comes from DummyJSON and what is simulated, plus the exact steps to get real Awin feeds (account → advertiser approval → feed URL → `.env` → import).
- **Importer validation** now also rejects:
  - implausible prices;
  - invalid or future timestamps;

  and it flags stale (old) timestamps.
- Nothing is scraped. No credentials or approvals were invented.

## Phase 4 — Price history ✅
- **Recording:** only real observations are recorded. Live mode uses the import history (`pa_catalog_price_history`). Test mode records each sample refresh: on a price change, or every 20 h at most.
- **Chart:** step chart per shop with 30 d / 90 d / 1 y / All. With fewer than 2 points at least 1 h apart, it shows "Not enough history yet" instead of a line.
- **Labels:** item price and delivered total are labelled separately, and FX-converted points are marked as approximate.
- **Tests:** unit tests plus a database test.

## Phase 5 — Price-drop alerts ✅ in this browser (⛔ email needs accounts, an email provider and a scheduler)
- **Managing alerts:** watch a product with a target delivered total; view, pause, edit and delete on `/alerts`.
- **When an alert fires:**
  - only complete totals count;
  - one notification per drop, re-armed if the price goes back above the target;
  - in-app inbox and toasts, with an on/off preference.
- **Email:** what it would need is described in `docs/ALERTS.md`. No email is sent, and the site does not claim otherwise.

## Phase 6 — Photo and barcode search ✅ (free, on-device)
- **Photo input:** upload, camera, drag-and-drop, preview, crop, replace and remove. Only JPEG/PNG/WebP up to 10 MB are accepted, and the photo never leaves the device.
- **Matching:** colour and shape similarity against the catalogue photos. Results are labelled "Visually similar", with High/Medium/Low and a %; below the threshold the page says "No visually similar product found".
- **Barcode:** gives an exact match (EAN/UPC check digit validated). `BarcodeDetector` reads barcodes from photos where the browser supports it.
- **Accuracy and cost:** tested with real catalogue photos (5/5 in the top 3). The limits and the paid upgrade options are in `docs/PHOTO_SEARCH.md`; nothing paid is used.

## Phase 7b — Interface languages ✅ English + Swedish, 🧪 German + Thai (beta)
- **Message files:** `src/i18n/messages/{en,sv,de,th}.ts`, about 450 keys each, type-checked so no key can be missing. A test checks that every placeholder matches.
- **Switching language:** the language selector in the header and the phone menu sets the `pv_locale` cookie and reloads. Server pages use `getT()`, client components `useT()`, and `<html lang>` follows the choice.
- **What is translated:**
  - header, footer and tab bar;
  - homepage, search, categories and departments;
  - product page (offer table, breakdown, gallery, price history);
  - compare, Local Deal Radar, location search and area dialog;
  - favourites, alerts, photo search, account and 404.
- **Formats:** numbers, prices and dates follow the language (e.g. "1 299,00 SEK" in Swedish). Country and currency names come from the browser.
- **Not translated (labelled on the page):** About, Stores & data sources and Price history information pages, admin, and product data itself (names, descriptions, specs from feeds). Category and sub-category names inside departments are English.
- **German and Thai** are machine-assisted and marked **(Beta)** until a native speaker reviews them. Only English and Swedish are called complete.
