# PriceViva v0.7 — progress log

v0.7 was built in stages on the same project:
- **Part 1:** photography, categories, header and footer, search price filter.
- **Parts 2–5:** everything else, listed below.

The release is one package: `priceviva-v0.7.zip`.

## Part 2 — Comparison and discovery ✅
- **Phase 5 · Global Compare:**
  - inline delivery-country selector;
  - offer ordering: delivered total (recommended), item price, fastest delivery;
  - item-price ordering explains that it excludes shipping and import costs;
  - original currency always shown; converted totals marked ≈; unknown costs never counted as zero; demo data labelled.
- **Phase 6 · Side-by-side comparison:**
  - add up to 3 products, with Remove and Clear all;
  - "Add another … product" link;
  - first column stays visible while swiping on phones;
  - empty and error states.
- **Phase 7 · Local Deal Radar:**
  - towns, **municipalities** (e.g. "Mönsterås kommun") and postcodes, with a unit test;
  - list/map views;
  - area and radius persist;
  - offers are tagged with the area they belong to, so a changed area never shows the previous area's offers;
  - graceful load errors;
  - mobile layout checked.
- **Phase 8 · Photo and barcode search:** upload, camera, preview, crop, type and size validation, barcode check digit. Photo matching is labelled "visually similar"; only a barcode gives an exact match. Re-tested 12/12.

## Part 3 — Favourites, alerts, accounts, languages, currencies ✅ (accounts ⛔)
- **Phase 9 · Favourites:** save/remove, persistence, empty state, export/import.
- **Phase 10 · Alerts:**
  - create/edit/pause/delete;

  - a Swedish scan of 10 main pages found no untranslated interface text;

- **Phase 13 · Currencies:** 10 currencies, number format follows the language, rate date shown, converted prices marked as estimates, delivery country never changed by currency.

## Part 4 — Contact, trust, responsive, accessibility ✅
- **Phase 14 · Contact:**
  - new `/contact` page and footer link;
  - the address comes only from `CONTACT_EMAIL` and must look like a real email (tested);
  - while it is empty the page says no address has been published yet;
  - no form and no email backend; nothing invented.
- **Phase 15 · Trust pages:**
  - About has a new **"What works today"** section listing what is working, what is demonstration-only (depends on mode) and what is not available;
  - Privacy policy and Disclosures are linked from the footer.
- **Phase 16 · Responsive:**
  - page headers now show the photo as a band above the text below 1024 px, so text no longer covers the people in the photos;
  - main pages checked at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px;
  - no horizontal overflow on 26 routes.
- **Phase 17 · Accessibility:**
  - new automated audit (`scripts/preview/a11y-audit.mjs`) on 14 pages × 2 widths × 3 data modes: one h1, no skipped heading levels, alt text, accessible names, labelled fields, no positive tabindex. Result: no issues;
  - search results gained a hidden "Search results" heading;
  - dark-mode buttons were below AA contrast (3.1:1) and now reach 4.7–4.8:1.

## Part 5 — Security, performance, SEO, testing ✅
- **Phase 18 · SEO:**
  - search engines are blocked unless you set `ALLOW_INDEXING=true` **and** real (live) data is on (tested);
  - `robots.txt` and a sitemap follow that switch;
  - pages have titles and descriptions;
  - the hero image loads first and category images load lazily.
- **Phase 19 · Security:**
  - no secrets in the repository; only the public Supabase anon key is public;
  - extra security headers: COOP, HSTS on HTTPS, a stricter Permissions-Policy;
  - external links use `noopener`;
  - stored and imported data is validated;
  - the contact address is validated.
  - The dependency audit (`npm audit`) could not run here.
- **Phase 20 · Honest status:** every feature is working, labelled as demonstration data, or listed as not available (About → "What works today").
